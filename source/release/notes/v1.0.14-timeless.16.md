Welcome to Timeless XI, a level 75 FINAL FANTASY XI private server. This is our own client, built for the server, and it keeps itself up to date.

### A new look

- A new title screen: a slow walk through Lufaise Meadows and the Hall of the Gods, with logging in in the same scene.
- Select Character in the Hall of the Gods, with your characters standing on the statue room's platform.
- A new Create Character, with hair colors (including two-tone), eye colors, face markings and lip colors.
- Reworked, higher-detail faces.
- Timeless View: a refreshed interface with clear, high-detail icons and the Timeless colors.

### Play your way

- Hotbars: three bars of twelve on the number keys, Ctrl and Alt, set up separately for each job. Right-click a slot to fill it.
- Fishing is built into the game, with the Angler's guide to every fish, zone and school.
- Companions fight alongside you, with their own windows to manage them.

### The world

- Lamps, lanterns and torches light up the night.
- Snowfalls and blizzards, with visible breath in the cold.
- Softer zone changes.

### New in Build 16

- Two-tone hair is smoother and more natural: bangs and the locks in front of the ears keep your main colour, and the back fades evenly.
- Hume female hairstyle 1: the ponytail takes your second colour, blending softly into the hair where it's tied.
- No more thin line down the parting.

### New in Build 15

- Faces: no more dark patch at the front of the neck or thin lines across the crown and forehead.
- Hair colour no longer tints the eyelids.
- Light and blonde hair now shows the same fine strands as dark hair.
- Mithra ears are their own again, free of hair and hair colour, and the strip between them takes your hair colour.
- Two-tone hair now fades evenly from the roots to the ends, ponytails included.
- Hair that kept its old colour (some Hume, Tarutaru and Mithra styles, and between headband stripes) now takes the colour you pick.

### New in Build 14

- The Timeless Aquarium is now a true show piece: a much larger cabinet with gilded trim, fluted columns, glowing crystal finials and a sunburst crest. The Elder fish you have caught swim around the tank on their own paths, and Rimecoil slithers like a true eel.
- More refined face markings and looks for Hume, Elvaan and Mithra characters.

### Fixed in Build 13

- The Angler's guide shows the server's new Perfect catch skill-up chance (50%).

- Mithra custom looks now use the real high-detail face: hair, eyes and features match the face you picked, instead of a washed-out stand-in.
- A Mithra's own face markings step aside when you design a custom look, so the markings you choose are the ones you see.

### Fixed in Build 12

- You're no longer sent back to the title with "Overlapped I/O operation is in progress" while playing.

See you in Vana'diel.
