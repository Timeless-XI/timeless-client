Welcome to Timeless XI, a level 75 FINAL FANTASY XI private server. This is our own client, built for the server, and it keeps itself up to date.

### A new look

- A new title screen: a slow walk through Lufaise Meadows and the Hall of the Gods, with logging in in the same scene.
- Select Character in the Hall of the Gods, with your characters standing on the statue room's platform.
- A new Create Character, with hair colors (including two-tone), eye colors, face markings and lip colors.
- Reworked, higher-detail faces.
- Timeless View: a refreshed interface with clear, high-detail icons and the Timeless colors.

### Play your way

- Hotbars: three bars of twelve on the number keys, Ctrl and Alt, set up separately for each job. Right-click a slot to fill it.
- Fishing is built into the game, with the Angler's guide to every fish, zone and school.
- Companions fight alongside you, with their own windows to manage them.

### The world

- Lamps, lanterns and torches light up the night.
- Snowfalls and blizzards, with visible breath in the cold.
- Softer zone changes.

See you in Vana'diel.
